原三模态 Demo 运行命令
========================

一、启动
终端 A：
  cd ~/p4-multimodal
  make

终端 B：
  cd ~/p4-multimodal
  python3 mycontroller.py

二、IPv4
mininet> pingall
*** Ping: testing ping reachability
h1 -> h2
h2 -> h1
*** Results: 0% dropped (2/2 received)

三、IPv6
mininet> h1 ping6 -c 3 fe80::5678%eth0
3 packets transmitted, 3 received, 0% packet loss, ttl=60

mininet> h2 ping6 -c 3 fe80::1234%eth0
3 packets transmitted, 3 received, 0% packet loss, ttl=60

四、Source Routing
mininet> xterm h1 h2
h2 xterm: python3 receive.py
h1 xterm: python3 send-sr.py 10.0.2.2
  输入：4 2 2 1
  输入：q
h2 xterm: python3 send-sr.py 10.0.1.1
  输入：4 1 1 1
  输入：q
实际输出：h2 收到解封装 IPv4/UDP，TTL=60
（反向也收到一份）

五、清理
mininet> exit
make stop
make clean
